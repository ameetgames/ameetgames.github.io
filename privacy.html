Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Ameet Games (ameetgames.github.io) is a free website of small educational sports games made by Ameet. This page explains what information the site uses and where it goes. The short version: your progress stays in your own browser, and the site has no server or database that collects your data.

What is saved, and where

The site saves your XP points, your daily streak and which games you have tried. This is saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never sent to Ameet or to any server run by Ameet Games. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. You can play every game and earn points without it. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your name and profile picture with the site. They are used only to show who is signed in and to keep a separate save for your account, and they are stored only in your browser, together with your points. The site does not ask for your email address, contacts, files or any other Google data, and never posts anything to your account.

Google's own handling of your sign-in is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Ameet Games' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What the site does not do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.

Hosting and online game modes

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Some games have online or "unblocked" versions that connect to a separate game server to run multiplayer matches. Those servers only pass game moves between players and do not receive your Google sign-in information.

Students and teachers

Ameet Games is meant to be safe for school use. Because no personal information leaves the player's browser, the site does not collect personal information from children. Students can play without signing in at all.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, sign out and clear this site's data in your browser settings (or clear your browsing data). If you signed in with Google, you can also remove the site's access from your Google account using the link above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
