2K OVR Calculations

Every player in Mini2K has an overall rating (OVR) from 25 to 99, and it is key to the game: it decides how good each player is on the court and how strong each team is when you pick a matchup. Instead of making up numbers, OVR is calculated from real box-score stats. It starts from a player's efficiency per 36 minutes, adjusts for position, sample size and playing time, and gives a bonus to players with a standout specialty like defense, three-point shooting or passing.
