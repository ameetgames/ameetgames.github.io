Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Ameet Games (ameetgames.github.io) is a free website of small educational sports games made by Ameet. This page explains what information the site uses and where it goes. The short version: if you don't sign in, everything stays in your own browser. If you sign in with Google, your first name, picture and points are saved online so they can appear on the public leaderboard, and you can hide yourself or delete it all at any time.

Playing without signing in

Signing in is optional. You can play every game and earn XP without it. Without signing in, your XP points, daily streak and the games you have tried are saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to Ameet Games, and clearing your browser data deletes it.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in, the site uses Google sign-in through Google Firebase, and your save is stored online in Firebase instead of only in your browser. This is what's stored:

  • Shown publicly on the leaderboard: your first name (only the first word of your Google name), your Google profile picture and your XP.
  • Also stored, but not shown on the leaderboard: your daily streak, the date you last visited and which games you have tried. Someone with technical know-how could read these alongside your leaderboard entry. If you hide yourself, they're linked only to a random ID, not to your name or picture.
  • Kept private by Firebase Authentication: your Google account ID and email address, which Google uses to manage sign-in. These are never shown on the site or shared.

The site does not access your contacts, files, Gmail or any other Google data, and never posts anything to your account. Google's handling of your sign-in is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Hiding yourself from the leaderboard

Signed-in players can choose "Hide me from the leaderboard" on the Leaderboard page or from the account menu. Hiding removes your name and picture from the online record and takes you off the leaderboard. Your XP keeps saving, and you can show yourself again at any time.

What the site does not do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.

Hosting and online game modes

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Some games have online or "unblocked" versions that connect to a separate game server to run multiplayer matches. Those servers only pass game moves between players and do not receive your Google sign-in information.

Students and teachers

Ameet Games is meant to be safe for school use. Students can play everything without signing in, and then no personal information leaves their browser. If a student signs in, their first name and Google picture appear on the public leaderboard unless they choose "Hide me from the leaderboard". Teachers who prefer students not to appear publicly can ask them to stay signed out or to hide themselves.

Deleting your data

  • Signed in: on the Leaderboard page, choose "Delete my online data". This permanently deletes your online save, your leaderboard entry and your Firebase sign-in record.
  • Not signed in: clear this site's data in your browser settings (or clear your browsing data).
  • You can also remove Ameet Games' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes
