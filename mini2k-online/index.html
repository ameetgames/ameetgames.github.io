Network settings (for school or work wifi)

Most home connections link the two computers directly. School and office wifi often blocks that; then the game needs a relay (a TURN server) that both computers can reach. A free Metered account gives 20 GB a month, which is hundreds of games (one game uses roughly 30 to 60 MB). Only the person who creates the room needs to fill this in; the friend who joins gets it automatically.
